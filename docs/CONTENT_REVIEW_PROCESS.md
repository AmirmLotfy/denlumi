# Protocol content review gate

All bundled Denlumi protocols remain `demo_only`. An owner reviewing a fictional plan is not a professional content approval. No practical guidance for real animals is published by this process document.

For each proposed live protocol version, the product owner must identify a qualified reviewer whose current credentials and relevant cat/dog behavior scope are verified outside Denlumi. Record the reviewer’s consent to attribution, credential source and verification date, conflicts, species/scenario scope, and a private contact path. Do not create a reviewer account or mark a credential verified from self-description alone.

Freeze the exact protocol bytes and calculate their digest before review. The review packet must include the applicable species pairing and newcomer direction, scenario and support exclusions, locale, source citations and permissions, prerequisites, stop/hold triggers, uncertainty wording, handover text, and every step that could be read aloud. A reviewer decision applies only to that digest, locale and scope. Any edit creates a new version and needs a new decision. The reviewer must explicitly approve, request changes, or reject it; silence is not approval.

The restricted publishing workflow must verify an active reviewer identity and authorization again at decision time, store the dated signed decision and expiry, and audit the actor and exact digest. Expired or revoked approval fails closed. A withdrawal must invalidate bound plans and sessions and cancel stale queued prompts; the local worker test covers this software behavior, while real operator, hosted and notification drills remain open. The reviewer must be able to withdraw an exact version without rewriting history.

The release operator must independently confirm that all required review records, source licenses, locale coverage, safety policy, privacy and accessibility gates are satisfied before enabling any live use. Until an actual reviewer is engaged and verified, the administrative approval/publishing path stays unavailable and all current examples stay fictional.
